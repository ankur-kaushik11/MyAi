Honey Chilli Potatoes Recipe Guide  Popular Variations  1   Classic Street Style: Deep-fried potatoes tossed in a flavorful sauce made with garlic, red chili sauce, tomato ketchup, honey, and vinegar.  2   Extra Crispy Version: Potatoes are coated in cornflour and chili sauce before frying, then tossed in a spicy garlic-chili sauce.  3   Oven-Roasted Version: A healthier alternative where potatoes are roasted at 200°C for 40–50 minutes before being coated in sauce.  Key Steps for Success  1   Prep: Peel and cut potatoes into uniform thick batons. Soak in cold water for 20–30 minutes to remove excess starch.  2   Coat & Fry: Dry thoroughly, coat with cornflour or maida, and deep fry until golden. Fry twice for extra crispiness.  3   Prepare Sauce: Sauté garlic, ginger, and red chilies. Add chili sauce, soy sauce, ketchup, and honey.  4   Toss: Add a cornflour slurry to thicken the sauce, then immediately toss in the fried potatoes.  Recipe Notes & Tips  1   Do not overcook after adding honey to maintain crispiness.  2   Serve immediately to avoid sogginess.  3   Adjust spice level by increasing or decreasing chili.  4   Add capsicum and onions for extra flavor and texture.
